Questions

Before you book the call

Do we need to be an approved Hadaf provider for our courses to qualify for subsidies?

Yes. Hadaf's own program terms state that only listed, accredited training providers are supported. A course from a non-listed provider does not qualify, regardless of quality.

What happens if we miss an HRDF invoice resubmission deadline?

Under Hadaf's Skills Development program terms, a rejected invoice must be resubmitted within a set window, with a capped number of attempts. Once that is exhausted, the claim is generally forfeited.

How fast do we need to turn around certificates and reports for corporate clients?

It varies by contract, but structured KSA workforce training agreements commonly ask for delivery within a few business days of grades being approved.

How long does it take to switch to SimpliTrain?

Most teams running SimpliTrain as a layer over an existing LMS go live in 4 to 6 weeks. A full rollout across TMS, LMS, and multiple centers typically takes 8 to 12 weeks, with the implementation team handling data migration and trainer onboarding.

Can we manage multiple training centers from one account?

Yes. SimpliTrain is built for multi-location and multi-tenant operations, with center-level permissions and roll-up reporting.

Does SimpliTrain handle both classroom (ILT) and online (VILT) training?

Yes. SimpliTrain runs instructor-led training (ILT), virtual instructor-led training (VILT), and self-paced e-learning from one system, with native integration for Zoom, Microsoft Teams, and Adobe Connect.

Is SimpliTrain available in Arabic?

Yes, alongside English.
